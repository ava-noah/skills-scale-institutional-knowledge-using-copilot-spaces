# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides a centralized index of OctoAcme's project management process documentation and a concise overview of how projects are run from initiation through continuous improvement.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lifecycle-based, iterative project management approach:

- **Initiation**: Validate business need, define goals and success metrics, align stakeholders, and authorize planning. Work begins with a lightweight one-pager covering the problem statement, SMART goal, milestones, initial risks, and resource needs—projects only proceed when success criteria are clear and team capacity is confirmed.
- **Planning**: Build a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, estimate scope, map risks and dependencies, and set milestone-based release targets. Planning emphasizes shippable increments and explicit traceability between tasks and business goals.
- **Execution & Tracking**: Deliver in small increments through a predictable team cadence (standups, PM/PdM syncs, sprint demos) using project boards that track work from Backlog through QA to Done. PR and CI quality gates, linked issues, and required approvals enforce traceability and code quality throughout delivery.
- **Risk Management & Communication**: Maintain a risk register with likelihood/impact scoring, review risks weekly, and escalate blockers through defined paths—from team-level triage to PM/Product Lead and sponsor-level escalation for business-critical issues. Regular status updates keep stakeholders informed and aligned.
- **Release & Deployment**: Enforce pre-release checks (CI/security scans, acceptance completion, release notes, rollback plan), execute staged deployments with smoke testing and post-deploy verification, and communicate release outcomes to stakeholders. Rollback and incident playbooks are prepared before every release.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**: Conduct retrospectives after sprints, releases, and incidents to capture lessons learned. Convert insights into owned, time-bound action items and track them to completion, reinforcing a culture of measurable, iterative process improvement.
